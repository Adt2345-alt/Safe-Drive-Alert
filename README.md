SafeDrive AI is an intelligent driver assistance and road infrastructure intelligence system that uses a dashcam and deep learning (YOLOv8) to detect potholes and speed bumps in real time, enhanced with sensor fusion (camera + IMU), monocular depth estimation for accurate distance measurement, lane awareness, and Time-to-Collision alerts. The system goes far beyond detection: it logs every defect with GPS coordinates, visualizes them on an interactive HD map with heatmap, trajectory, coverage, and predictive hotspot layers, and lets users inspect individual defects in 3D through a Digital Twin reconstruction view. On top of this, it includes a time-series analytics dashboard, an automated cost and damage calculator, a PDF work order generator for municipalities, multi-vehicle fleet management, role-based access control with audit logging, and a Safe Route Advisor that ranks driving routes by pothole risk instead of just speed. Built with Flask, React, MapLibre, Deck.gl, Three.js, scikit-learn, and OpenRouteService, SafeDrive AI turns raw dashcam footage into actionable road infrastructure intelligence — alerting drivers in real time while helping cities plan repairs, reduce vehicle damage costs, and prioritize maintenance budgets.
